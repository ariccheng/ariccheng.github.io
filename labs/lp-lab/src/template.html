An interactive guide to linear programming, worked through one small snowboard workshop.

Every allowed plan

The shaded floor holds every plan Ridgeline can make in a week. The height of the block above it is the weekly profit of each plan.

The highest point

Profit rises toward one corner. That corner, 5.25 Trail and 10.5 Summit boards, earns $945, the most of any allowed plan.

Seen from above

Looking straight down, the white lines across the block land on the floor as lines of equal profit. This flat view is the graph used in every lesson below.

Ridgeline Boards

A small workshop builds two snowboards. Each week it decides how many of each to make, within the hours it has.

Trail boardSummit boardAvailable per week
Profit per board$50$65
Fabrication3.5 h4 h84 h
Finishing1 h1.5 h21 h

Ridgeline also makes at least twice as many Summit boards as Trail boards. Written as a linear program:

Max  50T + 65Sweekly profit
s.t.  3.5T + 4S ≤ 84fabrication hours
   T + 1.5S ≤ 21finishing hours
   −2T + S ≥ 0mix rule, S ≥ 2T
   T, S ≥ 0
