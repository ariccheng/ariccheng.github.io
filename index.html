Aric Cheng

👋🏼 Greetings! I'm Aric Cheng (程宇清), a Ph.D. candidate in the NASPO Department of Supply Chain Management at W. P. Carey School of Business, Arizona State University. I am fortunate to be advised by Avnet Professor Elliot Rabinovich. I earned my Master's degree in Business Analytics from Washington University in St. Louis in 2023 and my Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics in 2021.

Digital Infrastructure Platform Operations Last-Mile Delivery Causal Inference Applied Econometrics

I study digital infrastructure in operations — how it is produced, maintained, and consumed, and how its quality shapes operational outcomes. My work combines large-scale archival data with quasi-experimental identification strategies. See Research for current projects.
